Why replace the factory cold side pipe

On 2011–2016 6.7L Powerstrokes, the factory cold side charge pipe that runs from the intercooler to the intake is plastic. Heat cycles and boost pressure can crack it or push the coupler off. When that happens the truck loses boost, drops power and can go into limp mode.

This kit replaces the plastic pipe with mandrel-bent aluminum tubing. The 4-ply silicone couplers and heavy-duty spring clamps hold the joints tight under boost. It's a common fix for trucks that come in with a boost leak, and a good preventive upgrade when the truck is already apart for other intake work.
